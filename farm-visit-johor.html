At the Farm

What You Will See

Bani Ibrahim Farms is a real, active livestock farm. This is not a staged attraction. Everything you see is part of daily farm operations.

Sheep and goats roam on open land. Dorper sheep are grass-fed on napier grass. No grain, no dedak. Boer and Kacang goats are raised free-range. You will see how animals eat, how they behave in a natural environment, and how a halal farm manages its livestock day to day.

For school groups, the visit is paired with a guided explanation of halal livestock standards and what makes a farm Syariah-compliant.

Your Visit

What Happens During a Farm Visit

A typical 1 to 2 hour visit follows this flow.

1

Arrival and Welcome

Arrive at the farm gate in Gunung Pulai. Free parking for cars, vans, and buses. Our team meets you and gives a brief orientation on farm rules and safety.

2

Guided Farm Tour

Walk through the farm areas. See how Dorper sheep and Boer goats are kept, fed, and managed. Our team explains the differences between breeds and how animals are selected for aqiqah and qurban.

3

Animal Interaction

Kids and adults can get close to the animals. Feed the goats and sheep under supervision. This is the highlight for family groups. Animals are calm and accustomed to visitors.

4

Farm Education (Schools and Corporates)

School groups receive a structured session on halal livestock farming, Syariah requirements, and sustainable farming practices. Corporate groups can include a team activity segment here.

5

Q and A and Debrief

Open Q and A with our farm team. Ask anything about how we raise animals, our aqiqah and qurban services, or how to buy live animals. Schools can relate this to their curriculum topics.
