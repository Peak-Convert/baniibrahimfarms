Understanding Qurban

What is Qurban?

Qurban (also written as korban or kurban) is the Islamic act of sacrificing a livestock animal during Eid al-Adha — the Festival of Sacrifice. It commemorates Prophet Ibrahim's (AS) willingness to sacrifice his son Ismail as an act of complete submission to Allah, before Allah replaced the sacrifice with a ram.

Qurban is performed on the 10th, 11th, or 12th of Zulhijjah. It is an act of worship that combines gratitude, generosity, and remembrance — making it one of the most significant ibadah of the Islamic calendar.

The meat is divided into three equal portions: one for your family, one for relatives and friends, and one for the poor and needy. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we handle the distribution to those in need and document everything for you.

Qurban is a highly recommended Sunnah (Sunnah Muakkadah) for every Muslim who is financially able to do so. It is not compulsory (fardhu), but scholars strongly encourage those who are financially capable to perform it.
