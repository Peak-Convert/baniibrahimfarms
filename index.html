What You Need to Know

Common Questions, Clear Answers

What is Aqiqah?

Aqiqah is a Sunnah practice in Islam where a goat is slaughtered to celebrate the birth of a child — typically within 7 days, though it can be done at any age. Two goats are recommended for a boy, one for a girl. The meat is shared with family and distributed to those in need. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we handle the entire process — slaughter, distribution, and documentation — so you can fulfil this Sunnah with ease.

What is Qurban?

Qurban (also called Korban) is the Islamic ritual of sacrificing a livestock animal during Eid al-Adha (10th–13th Zulhijjah). It commemorates the sacrifice of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and is an act of worship and gratitude. One goat covers one person's qurban; one cow covers up to seven. The meat is divided into three parts — for the family, relatives, and those in need. Bani Ibrahim Farms performs qurban in Gunung Pulai, Johor, with full Syariah compliance and photo documentation.

Why Choose a Halal Certified Farm?

For aqiqah and qurban to be valid under Islamic law, the animal must be slaughtered by a Muslim, with the correct niyyah (intention), and in accordance with halal requirements. A certified halal farm ensures the animal is healthy, well-raised, and slaughtered correctly — giving you full confidence that your ibadah is accepted. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, every slaughter is documented and transparent.
