Understanding Qurban

What is Qurban?

Qurban (also written as korban or qurbani) is the act of slaughtering a livestock animal during Eid al-Adha in remembrance of the sacrifice of Prophet Ibrahim (AS). It is a highly recommended Sunnah for every Muslim who has the means to perform it.

Qurban is performed on the 10th, 11th, 12th, or 13th of Zulhijjah, the last month of the Islamic calendar. Each slaughter must be done after the Eid prayer and before sunset on the 13th of Zulhijjah.

The meat is distributed in three parts: one-third for the family, one-third for relatives, and one-third for those in need. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we manage the full distribution and document everything.
