Feed Makes the Difference

Why Our Feed Matters

Some farms raise livestock cheaply on dedak alone. We grow our own feed. Here is what that means for the meat you get.

🌿

Farm-Grown Feed

Our Dorper sheep eat napier and other grasses, greens and corn grown on our own farm. Not a cheap, dedak-only diet.

🌽

Farm-Made Silage

We mix our grasses, corn and greens and ferment them into silage, so the animals eat well and get balanced nutrition all year round.

🚫

No Routine Antibiotics

We do not administer routine antibiotics. Animals are treated only when medically required. This is standard halal farming practice and reduces the risk of antibiotic residues in meat.

🍖

No Gamey Smell. Cleaner Flavour.

Good nutrition gives meat without the strong gamey smell common in animals raised on low-quality feed. Cleaner-tasting meat that needs less masking in cooking.

Islamic Requirements

Syariah Requirements for Aqiqah and Qurban Animals

Every animal we sell meets these requirements. We guide you through the selection process.

  • ✓ Animal is healthy and free of significant physical defects
  • ✓ Minimum age met: sheep or goat must be at least 1 year old for qurban
  • ✓ No blindness, lameness, or severe illness that affects the animal's condition
  • ✓ Animal is well-fed and in good body condition at time of purchase
  • ✓ Halal slaughter performed by a trained Muslim with correct niyyah
  • ✓ All animals raised without practices that contravene Islamic principles
  • ✓ Farm operates under Syariah-compliant animal management standards
  • ✓ We advise on selection. Tell us your purpose and we match the right animal.
