Understanding Aqiqah

What is Aqiqah?

Aqiqah is a Sunnah practice in Islam where a goat or sheep is slaughtered to celebrate the birth of a child. The Prophet Muhammad (SAW) performed aqiqah for his grandchildren Hassan and Hussain, making it a highly recommended act of worship.

Aqiqah is ideally performed on the 7th, 14th, or 21st day after the child's birth. If not done at birth, it remains valid and is highly encouraged at any point in the child's life — or even for adults who never had it done.

The meat from aqiqah is traditionally divided: one-third for the family, one-third for relatives, and one-third for those in need. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we handle the full distribution and provide you with photo and video proof.
