Our Story

A Farm Built on
Faith and Family

Bani Ibrahim Farms is a family-run halal livestock farm in Gunung Pulai, Johor. Over 5 years, 1,000+ families. Every aqiqah and qurban performed with the same honesty we would want for our own children.

Named after the Prophet Ibrahim (AS), whose willingness to sacrifice is the very foundation of aqiqah and qurban, this farm was built on one principle: do things correctly, or do not do them at all.

Who We Are

Bani Ibrahim Farms is a family-run halal livestock farm located in the highlands of Gunung Pulai, Johor. We have been operating for over 5 years, performing aqiqah and qurban for families across Malaysia and serving local customers with live goat sales, fresh vegetables, and farm visits.

We are not a middleman company. We are the farm. When you WhatsApp us, you speak directly to the people who raise the animals, perform the slaughter, and distribute the meat. No call centres. No outsourcing.

Why Gunung Pulai

Our farm sits in the highlands of Gunung Pulai, Johor. The cooler climate, clean air, and open land give our goats the space and conditions to be raised naturally. No crowded feedlots. No artificial shortcuts. The animals graze, move freely, and are fed on natural feed. This is not a marketing claim. It is the only way we know how to farm.

How We Operate

✅

Syariah-Compliant. Every Time.

Every slaughter is performed by a trained Muslim professional. Proper niyyah recited. Full Islamic rites. Every single time. No exceptions.

Photo and Video Proof

We send you photo and video documentation of your aqiqah or qurban on the same day it is performed. You see exactly what was done on your behalf.

Naturally Raised Goats

Our goats are raised on natural feed in the Gunung Pulai highlands. Open space. No crowded pens. No shortcuts on animal welfare.

Direct from the Farm

No agents. No middlemen. You deal directly with the farm. Honest pricing. Clear communication. No surprises.

Giving Back to the Community

After every aqiqah and qurban, meat is distributed to orphans and underprivileged families in the Gunung Pulai area. Your ibadah reaches those who need it most.
