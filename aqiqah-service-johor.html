Understanding Aqiqah

What is Aqiqah?

Aqiqah is a Sunnah practice in Islam where a goat or sheep is slaughtered to celebrate the birth of a child. It is one of the most recommended acts of worship a parent can perform for their child.

The ideal time is the 7th, 14th, or 21st day after birth. If not done at birth, it remains valid at any age. Adult aqiqah is recognised and accepted in Islamic jurisprudence.

The meat is distributed to those in need. At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we distribute to orphans and underprivileged families in Johor. You receive photo and video documentation as proof.
