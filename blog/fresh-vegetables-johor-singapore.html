Why Singaporeans Drive to Johor for Fresh Produce

The price difference is real. Fresh vegetables in Johor typically cost 30 to 50 percent less than equivalent produce in Singapore supermarkets. But price is only part of the reason more families are making the trip. The bigger draw is quality and traceability.

When you buy vegetables at a supermarket, you do not know where they were grown, when they were harvested, or what was applied to them. When you buy directly from a farm, you can ask. You can see the soil. Your children can see where food comes from.

That is the difference between a grocery run to JB and a visit to Bani Ibrahim Farms.

Note on bringing food back to Singapore:

Fresh vegetables for personal consumption are generally permitted into Singapore from Malaysia. The Singapore Food Agency (SFA) regulates food imports — check sfa.gov.sg for the latest guidelines before your visit, as rules can change. Declare items at customs when in doubt. Commercial quantities require an import licence. Most family-sized farm purchases for personal use are not restricted.

Make It a Full Day: Gunung Pulai + Farm Visit

Gunung Pulai is already a known destination for Singaporeans. The Gunung Pulai Recreational Forest has walking trails, a waterfall, and clean air that is difficult to find an hour south of the Causeway. Our farm is in the same area.

3pm
