Some farms raise sheep cheaply on dedak alone. Ours eat a natural, nutritious diet that we grow ourselves: fresh-cut grass, farm-grown greens and our own corn, mixed together and made into silage. A well-fed sheep is a healthy sheep, and you can smell and taste the difference in the meat.

What Our Sheep Actually Eat

We do not buy in cheap feed and call it natural. Almost everything our sheep eat is grown on our own land in Gunung Pulai. Here is what goes into their diet.

🌾

Fresh-Cut Grass

Napier and other grasses we grow on the farm, cut fresh and brought to the sheep.

🌽

Our Own Corn and Greens

Corn and greens grown on our farm add energy and nutrition that grass alone cannot give.

🌿

Farm-Made Silage

We mix our grasses, corn and greens together and ferment them into silage, so the sheep eat well all year round.

Why Feed Matters

What an animal eats affects everything: its health, its meat, and its suitability for religious slaughter. A dedak-only diet is cheap, but it is low in nutrition. A varied, farm-grown diet gives the animal what it needs to grow healthy.

👃

No Gamey Smell

Good nutrition gives clean-smelling meat. No strong smell to cover up with spices.

🕌

Better for Aqiqah and Qurban

A healthy, well-fed animal is the right choice for a religious obligation.

😋

Nicer Taste

Meat from a well-fed animal tastes cleaner. Pepper and salt is often enough.

👁️

Transparency You Can Verify

Ask us. We will show you the crops, the silage, the animals and the farm. No secrets.

You Are What Your Food Ate

Think about two people. One eats home-cooked meals every day: rice, vegetables, fish, fruit. The other lives on burgers, fries, instant noodles and fizzy drinks because it is cheap and fills the stomach. Give it a few years and you can see the difference. One is fit and full of energy. The other is tired, unhealthy and does not look or feel good.

Sheep are no different. Dedak-only feed is the junk food of the farm: cheap, filling and low in nutrition. It grows the animal, but not well. Our sheep eat the farm version of a home-cooked meal: fresh-cut grass, greens and corn we grow ourselves. And what the sheep ate does not disappear after slaughter. It ends up in the meat on your plate. That is why our meat has no gamey smell and tastes cleaner.

🥗

The bottom line

You would not expect to stay healthy on junk food. Do not expect great meat from a sheep that ate the farm's junk food either. Good meat starts with good feed. That is why our meat has no gamey smell, and why we are happy to show you exactly what our sheep eat.
