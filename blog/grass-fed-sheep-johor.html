What "Grass-Fed" Actually Means Here

We do not use "grass-fed" as a marketing phrase. We grow napier grass on our own land, cut it daily, and bring it to the sheep. No pellets. No shortcuts. Here is exactly what that looks like.

🌾

Fresh-Cut Napier Grass Daily

We cut napier grass every day and bring it to our sheep. Not pellets. Not dedak. Real grass.

🌿

Napier Silage for Dry Season

We ferment napier into silage ourselves to ensure consistent grass-based feeding year-round. No seasonal shortcuts.

🚫

Zero Grains. Zero Dedak.

Our sheep have never been fed grain concentrates or dedak. This is not a marketing claim. It is how we have always farmed.

Why Grass-Fed Matters

What an animal eats affects everything: its health, its meat, and its suitability for religious slaughter. Grain-finished animals grow faster but accumulate residues from concentrates. Grass-fed animals grow at a natural pace. The difference is in the animal you are choosing for aqiqah or qurban.

🥩

Cleaner Meat

No grain residues. The animal ate only what nature intended.

🕌

Better for Aqiqah and Qurban

A grass-fed animal raised without shortcuts is the right choice for a religious obligation.

⚖️

Leaner, Better Flavour

Grass-fed meat is naturally leaner with a richer, cleaner flavour compared to grain-finished animals.

Grass-Fed Sheep for Aqiqah and Qurban in Johor

Syariah requires a healthy animal, free from obvious defects, for aqiqah and qurban to be valid. Our grass-fed sheep meet and exceed this standard. They grow at a natural pace, eat clean food, and are raised in open space at Gunung Pulai.

When you book aqiqah or qurban with us, you are choosing an animal that has been raised honestly. We perform the slaughter with proper niyyah and send you photo and video documentation to your email after slaughter.
