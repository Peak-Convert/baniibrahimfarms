Pesticide-Free Vegetables
40 Minutes from Singapore

No chemicals. No middlemen. Picked on the day you collect. Bani Ibrahim Farms in Gunung Pulai, Johor, grows vegetables without pesticides and sells direct from the farm. This is what clean produce looks like.

Many Singaporeans cross the Causeway for groceries. Most head to supermarkets in JB. But if you want vegetables that were growing in the ground yesterday — with zero pesticides — a farm in Gunung Pulai is worth the extra 20 minutes.

Why Pesticide-Free Matters

Most commercially grown vegetables — whether from Singapore supermarkets or wet markets — are treated with pesticides at some point in the supply chain. For everyday adults, the residue levels may be within regulated limits. But for families with young children, pregnant women, or anyone with health sensitivities, the question of how produce was grown matters.

At Bani Ibrahim Farms, we use no pesticides. The vegetables are grown using natural farming methods on highland soil in Gunung Pulai — the same farm where our halal goats roam. No chemical sprays. No synthetic fertilisers beyond what the land naturally provides.

SINGAPORE CUSTOMS NOTE

Fresh vegetables imported from Malaysia for personal consumption are permitted into Singapore. The Singapore Food Agency (SFA) allows personal importation of fresh produce from Malaysia.

Large commercial quantities require an import licence. For a typical family shop, you are fine. Declare at customs if asked. Always check sfa.gov.sg for the latest guidelines before your trip.
